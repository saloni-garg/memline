Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. Written in plain language on purpose.

What we store

Your email address and a hashed password (we cannot read your password). The understandings you approve, the proposals waiting in your inbox (including the evidence they came with, so you can judge them), and your ratings of how surprising they were, the context assemblies you generate, and basic usage counts used to apply your plan limits.

What we do not store

Files you import are processed to build proposed understandings and then discarded. We do not keep your raw ChatGPT or Claude exports. We do not store conversations you have with other assistants.

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your understandings to train models. We do not show them to other users. Your assistants can only reach them with your API key or your signed-in session, and anything an assistant suggests still needs your approval in your inbox.

Your controls

Read, edit, archive or reject any understanding at any time. Download everything we hold about you from Settings (Export my data). Delete your account from Settings and everything tied to it is erased. API keys can be revoked at any time, which cuts off assistant access immediately.

Cookies

One session cookie keeps you signed in. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers.

Questions or requests: hello@memline.app
